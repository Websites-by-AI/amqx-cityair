iPhone — iOS / iSO build

An iOS app (IPA) must be built and signed on a Mac with Xcode and an Apple developer account, so it is not in this release.

Until then, iPhone users can add CityAir to the home screen: open aqmx.atikova.com in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
