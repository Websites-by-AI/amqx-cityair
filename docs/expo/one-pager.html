The independent evidence layer for air-quality technology in cities

CityAir assesses how ready a city actually is to use air-quality technology — across twelve domains, with an evidence-confidence score — and publishes implementation briefs that state what a deployment needs, what it risks and what must be monitored. We do not sell sensors.

Six ways to work with us

1 · City pilot

You supply hardware or connectivity for one district; we supply the readiness baseline, the indicator set and the published result.

2 · Data & interoperability

Your operational data is mapped into the twelve domains, so a city's assessment cites real evidence instead of self-declared answers.

3 · Co-authored innovation brief

One product, one honest limitation, one reference deployment — published with full attribution in our open library.

4 · Sponsorship with a firewall

Fund an assessment cycle or a study. Sponsors never set scores and never edit findings. Named as funders only.

5 · Capacity building

Joint trilingual webinars and municipal workshops on evidence quality, procurement and risk communication.

6 · Independent evaluation

Six-month before/after evaluation with published method, uptime, drift and uncertainty — the strongest sales argument a vendor can hold.
